# Slop lexicon

Words and phrases that mark text as machine-written. Organized by where they show up so you can jump to the relevant section.

**The default fix is deletion, not substitution.** Most of these are filler; swapping in a synonym keeps the filler and adds a thesaurus smell. Replacements are given only where something real is missing.

## Corporate adjectives and nouns

Mostly delete. If the adjective is load-bearing, replace with the evidence.

**Adjectives:** robust, seamless, holistic, myriad, plethora, multifaceted, nuanced, intricate, pivotal, crucial, vital, transformative, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, best-in-class, world-class, next-level, innovative, dynamic, vibrant, rich (as in "rich history"), comprehensive, unparalleled, unprecedented, bespoke, curated, meaningful, impactful, actionable, scalable (when unquantified), synergistic

**Nouns:** tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, ecosystem, journey, arena, sphere, paradigm, synergy, framework (when vague), solution (as a product), offering, vertical, space (as in "the fintech space"), thought leadership, value-add, learnings, insights, takeaways, deliverables, touchpoint, north star, secret sauce, low-hanging fruit, deep dive, game plan

**The specific offender:** "valuable insights," "key takeaways," and "actionable insights" are near-certain AI markers. Cut on sight.

## Hedge stacks

Model prose hedges twice where once would do, or where zero would do.

- "It's possible that this might potentially..." → "This might..."
- "Some would argue that, in certain cases..." → make the claim
- "While there are many factors to consider..." → name the factor that matters
- "generally speaking, in most cases" → pick one
- "I think that perhaps we could maybe" → "We could"

Keep a hedge only when the uncertainty is real and load-bearing. "I'm not sure this generalizes past 50 engineers" is a good hedge. "It's worth considering that results may vary" is not.

## Words that are fine

Overcorrection is its own tell. These are normal English and don't need avoiding: use, help, make, build, think, want, good, hard, better, problem, work, thing, because, but, so, and starting a sentence with any of them. Contractions are fine everywhere except the most formal contexts. Sentence fragments are fine occasionally. Starting with "And" or "But" is fine.

The goal is prose that reads like a competent person typed it, not prose that has been scrubbed until it has no texture.
